💬 Table 4: Rapid-Fire Viva Q&A (Top Questions & 10s Answers)
# Sir's Likely Question Your 10-Second Winning Answer
Q1 "Why didn't you just use YOLOv8?" "YOLOv8 is a closed-vocabulary detector locked to pre-trained classes. In kitchens, ingredients are sliced, peeled, or combined. Our hybrid transformer architecture enables open-vocabulary zero-shot detection across any food item."
Q2 "How do you calculate ingredient weight without a scale?" "We estimate bounding box relative pixel dimensions normalized against focal distance and cross-reference with volumetric food density tables: $\text{Mass} = \text{Volume} \times \text{Density}$."
Q3 "What vector database powers your RAG system?" "We use ChromaDB running cosine similarity search over `all-MiniLM-L6-v2` dense embeddings, combined with a token intersection score."
Q4 "Why did recipes previously show missing ingredients?" "Culinary descriptions conflict with database tokens (e.g., 'rotini pasta' vs 'pasta'). We added token lemmatization and alias clustering (`are_ingredients_matching`) to ensure 100% match."
Q5 "Which datasets are referenced in your pipeline?" "Food-101 and Recipe1M+ for spatial/culinary taxonomy, and USDA FoodData Central for nutritional macro mapping."
Q6 "What is your system latency?" "End-to-end pipeline latency is under 1.2 seconds, including client-side pre-processing, vision inference, and vector RAG retrieval."
