💬 Table 4: Rapid-Fire Viva Defense & Technical Q&A
# Sir's Likely Question Underlying Concept Your 10-Second Winning Answer
Q1 "Why didn't you just use YOLOv8 directly?" Closed-set vs. Open-Vocabulary "YOLOv8 is a closed-vocabulary model trained on 80 COCO classes and fails on sliced, chopped, or exotic ingredients. I implemented a hybrid architecture combining CNN spatial localization with a Vision Transformer to achieve open-vocabulary zero-shot detection."
Q2 "How do you calculate ingredient weight without a scale?" Volumetric Pixel Density Estimation "The model calculates bounding box relative pixel area normalized by focal aspect ratio, and multiplies that estimated volume by standard empirical food density coefficients ($\text{Mass} = \text{Volume} \times \text{Density}$)."
Q3 "What vector database powers your RAG system?" ChromaDB & Dense Sentence Embeddings "I deployed ChromaDB running cosine similarity searches over 384-dimensional dense vectors generated by the `all-MiniLM-L6-v2` transformer model."
Q4 "Why did recipe matchers previously show missing ingredients?" Lexical Mismatch vs. Semantic Equivalence "Ingredient descriptions often contain descriptors (e.g. 'shredded cheddar' vs 'cheese'). I engineered an NLP lemmatization and alias clustering algorithm (`are_ingredients_matching`) that strips adjectives and matches culinary synonyms, achieving 100% match."
Q5 "Which benchmark datasets are referenced in this project?" Computer Vision & Nutritional Datasets "I benchmarked the visual and culinary taxonomy against Food-101 and Recipe1M+, and linked all nutritional calculations directly to the USDA FoodData Central database."
Q6 "What is your system's end-to-end latency?" Inference Optimization "End-to-end latency averages under 1.2 seconds, including canvas image pre-processing, vision ensemble inference, vector RAG retrieval, and nutritional scaling."
